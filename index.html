HOW I THINK / 03

Good design starts
with good questions.

I value a thoughtful process that connects what people need with what a product is trying to achieve.

01

Understand

Explore the context, listen to people, and define the problem before jumping to solutions.

02

Make sense

Look for patterns, connect insights, and turn observations into clear design opportunities.

03

Explore

Shape ideas through flows, wireframes, prototypes, and collaborative feedback.

04

Improve

Evaluate the experience, learn from what doesn't work, and refine with intention.
